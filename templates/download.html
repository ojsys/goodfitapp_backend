Version {{ ipa.version }} · {{ ipa.mb }} MB · {{ ipa.date }} · needs a Mac or Windows PC

Until we’re on TestFlight, iPhone testing takes about 10 minutes of setup on a computer, and the app has to be reinstalled every 7 days. If that’s too much, check back soon for a one-tap TestFlight link.

Download iPhone file (.ipa)
  1. On your computer, install Sideloadly from sideloadly.io (free). On Windows, also install iTunes from apple.com, not the Microsoft Store.
  2. Download the iPhone file above onto the computer.
  3. Plug your iPhone into the computer and tap Trust on the phone.
  4. Drag the file into Sideloadly, enter your Apple ID and press Start. Apple uses it to sign the app for your phone only.
  5. On the iPhone, turn on Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode and restart when asked.
  6. Then go to Settings → General → VPN & Device Management, tap your Apple ID and tap Trust.
  7. Open A Good Fit and sign up with your email.

This build stops opening after 7 days. Repeat step 4 to renew it, and your data stays. Sign in with Apple, notifications and Plus purchases don’t work in this build. Sideloadly is a third-party tool, so some testers use a spare Apple ID with it.
