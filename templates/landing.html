How it’s built

Show up. Look out. Keep it kind.

  • Private by defaultTracks are trimmed around home and the first and last 200 m on the server. Locations for matching are rounded to about a kilometer.
  • Built for bad signalSaves and messages carry a client id, so a retry from a tunnel or a dead zone never posts twice.
  • Safety in the APISlow mode, rate limits, blocks that work both ways, and a 21+ age check before member features open.
  • Plain and predictableJSON over HTTPS, meters and seconds, ISO times in UTC, and errors with a code you can switch on.
Quickstart

Your first call in a minute.

  1. Sign inEmail, Apple or Google. You get an access token and a refresh token.
  2. Send the tokenAs Authorization: Bearer … on every request. In the docs, use Authorize.
  3. Load HomeOne call returns the week, the feed, upcoming rides and suggestions.
